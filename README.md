# One-Line Office Jokes

Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-markk-st-one-line-office-jokes-a51f2bcc) bounty "One-Line Office Jokes".

`main.py` writes one single-line office joke, under 200 characters,
to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My boss calls it synergy when five of us do one person's job while that person is on annual leave.

Why it works: the boss's favourite buzzword, "synergy", is the thing
being mocked — five people doing one person's job is not teamwork, it
is the team covering for someone who is away, and everyone who has sat
through a synergy meeting recognises it instantly. "Five of us" keeps
the setup tight and puts the reader inside the overworked team, which
is where the laugh comes from.

Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Verified 25 Nov 2026: the line is 98 characters on one line, and two
runs produce byte-identical output (sha256 5a39c250...).
